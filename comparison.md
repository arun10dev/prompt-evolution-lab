# Image Comparison

## Version 1 — Baseline
- The image shows a wide-angle football stadium scene.
- It contains many stadium and crowd details.
- The overall appearance was judged somewhat artificial.
- The scoreboard contained incorrect or unintended team text when inspected.

## Version 2 — Specific Prompt
User observations:
- Team logos/emblems were clearer than in Version 1.
- Blue-team jerseys were more clearly represented.
- The blue theme was much more prominent.
- The scoreboard and team-related details made the match presentation more specific.
- Both Version 1 and Version 2 looked artificial.

## Version 3 — Context-Rich Prompt
User-confirmed observations:
1. **Stadium atmosphere:** Strong rivalry atmosphere with distinct fan sections.
2. **Home and away supporters:** Clearly distinguishable.
3. **Team kits:** White and blue-garnet kits are distinguishable.
4. **Stadium identity:** Stadium and match context are clearly represented.
5. **Realism:** Somewhat realistic, with visible AI-like details.
6. **Match visibility:** Both teams and the pitch are visible.

## Overall Findings
- Moving from a generic prompt to a more specific prompt made the requested theme and match details more prominent.
- Adding match and venue context helped represent the intended El Clásico scene, including contrasting supporter sections and kits.
- AI-like details remained, so greater prompt specificity did not fully resolve perceived realism.
- The wide-angle view showed the pitch and crowd, while individual players remained relatively small.

**Note:** Scoreboard text, scores, and match times are generated image details, not verified real-world match information.
